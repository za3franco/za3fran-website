Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  Za3fran Consulting

Za3fran Consulting ("Za3fran", "we", "us") operates the website za3fran.io and the Fond de Sauce editorial publication. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

Information we collect

We collect information only when you choose to share it with us, including:

  • Your name and email address if you subscribe to Fond de Sauce or contact us directly
  • Messages you send us via WhatsApp if you interact with our editorial automation tool
  • Basic usage data collected automatically by our hosting provider (Vercel) such as page views and referral sources — this data is aggregated and not linked to individuals

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Send you editorial content you have subscribed to
  • Respond to your messages and enquiries
  • Improve the quality and relevance of our content
  • Operate our internal editorial pipeline (topic suggestions submitted via WhatsApp are used solely to inform content planning)

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

WhatsApp integration

Za3fran uses the WhatsApp Business API to receive editorial topic suggestions. Messages sent to our WhatsApp number are processed by our automation system and stored securely in our database. This data is used exclusively for internal editorial planning and is not shared with third parties.

Data storage and security

Your data is stored on Supabase (EU-West region) and processed via n8n automation infrastructure hosted on Render. We take reasonable technical measures to protect your information from unauthorised access.

Cookies

Za3fran.io does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. Our hosting provider may set functional cookies necessary for site delivery.

Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Third-party services

We use the following third-party services to operate our platform:

Each of these providers operates under their own privacy policies.
