Questions

Do I need the Validator first?

Yes — the Validator is the intake form for the entire ecosystem. All downstream tools (Business Plan, Menu Engineer) pull from your Validator concept data. New users always start there. If you already have a report, use your access code to add tools.

Can I buy tools separately later?

Yes. If you've already done the Validator, you can add the Business Plan or Menu Engineer at any time using your access code. The loyalty rate applies when you already hold both the Validator and Business Plan.

What is a Menu regeneration?

Every Menu Engineer purchase includes one free regeneration — if you want to adjust the concept direction, menu style, or brand inputs, you can update your choices and regenerate all three deliverables. Additional regenerations are €49 each, triggered directly from your report.

What languages and currencies are supported?

All reports are generated in both English and French. Financial figures and pricing are displayed in your local currency — EUR, MAD, or USD — detected automatically and used throughout the report.

How are financial figures estimated?

Business Plan Essentials uses regional F&B benchmarks clearly labeled as estimates — not modeled from real data. The upcoming Financial Builder (Phase 6) will replace these with investor-grade modeled projections from your actual inputs.
